Why this, why now

Smartphones reached the village. The advice didn't.

Farmers across Bangladesh carry better cameras than most agri-officers did a decade ago. What's missing is trustworthy, Bangla-first advice that understands local crops, pests, prices and schemes. That's what we build — for agri-input companies, processors, FPOs, NGOs and government projects.

  • Leaf photos → specific disease & dose advice in Bangla
  • Drone & phone vision for plot health, weed pressure, crop count
  • Market-price LLMs that pull from actual haat reports
  • Traceability from field to FCI, mill or buyer

02 — Pipeline

From a photo of a leaf to a better season.

01 · INGEST
Phone, drone, sensor

Leaf photos, field videos, drone imagery, satellite passes, weather feeds, haat prices and farmer voice notes.

02 · PERCEIVE
Vision + speech

CV identifies crops, diseases, weeds, livestock; ASR transcribes Bangla speech; NLP extracts intent.

03 · REASON
Agronomy logic

Combine observations with agronomy rules, historic yield data and market signals to produce advice and forecasts.

04 · ACT
Advice in Bangla

Deliver plain-Bangla guidance over app, WhatsApp, IVR or SMS — optionally spoken aloud.

05 · LEARN
Closing the loop

Outcomes — yield, price, disease recovery — feed back into better models and better advice next season.
