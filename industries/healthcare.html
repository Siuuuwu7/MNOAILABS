Why this, why now

The bottleneck isn't expertise. It's paperwork, translation and time.

Bangladeshi clinicians see extraordinary volumes of patients. Most of the friction isn't diagnostic — it's dictating notes, explaining prescriptions, translating English instructions into Bangla, and chasing follow-ups. That's exactly where careful, clinician-supervised AI shines.

  • Always clinician-in-the-loop; AI never acts alone on clinical decisions
  • Private, on-prem deployment with full audit logs
  • Bangla-first for patients; English-friendly for clinicians
  • Built to complement your HIS / EMR / LIS, not replace them

02 — Pipeline

Private by default, clinician-supervised by design.

01 · INGEST
Prescriptions, notes, images

Scanned prescriptions, dictations, EMR notes, lab PDFs, radiology images, patient chats — all with consent & access logging.

02 · PERCEIVE
Vision + speech + NER

OCR, ASR, medical NER and imaging models extract structured clinical facts.

03 · REASON
Clinical logic

Protocols, drug databases and RAG over your own documents produce suggestions — with sources.

04 · ACT
Draft + clinician review

AI drafts notes, summaries, patient replies; clinicians review and sign. Nothing goes out unreviewed.

05 · AUDIT
Every prompt, every decision

Full audit trail of prompts, evidence, edits and final decisions — suitable for hospital compliance and regulators.
