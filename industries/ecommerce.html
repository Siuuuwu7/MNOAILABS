Bangla-first · WhatsApp-ready
Why this, why now

Bangladesh shops on Facebook, WhatsApp and a few marketplaces. Your AI should live there too.

The average Bangladeshi online store runs on two or three people answering DMs, shooting phone photos and writing captions at midnight. Gen-AI is the first technology that genuinely scales that work — if it speaks Bangla, understands Banglish and plays well with WhatsApp, Messenger and Daraz.

  • Auto product photos, videos and captions from a single phone shot
  • Banglish search that actually finds the product
  • WhatsApp and Messenger agents that close orders in Bangla
  • Gen-AI marketing that stays on brand in both languages
